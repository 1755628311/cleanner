5. الأذونات

The App requests permissions only when needed for specific features. You may grant or deny permissions in Android system settings. Denying a permission may limit related functionality.

  • Storage / Media access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_*) — scan and clean cache, temporary files, and media content
  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — deep cleaning where scoped storage limits regular access
  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) — show per-app usage and cache statistics
  • Network (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, INTERNET) — check connectivity; load Privacy Policy and Terms in-app via WebView
  • Wi‑Fi state (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE) — display Wi‑Fi-related information on the System Info screen
  • Request delete packages (REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES) — assist with uninstalling apps you choose to remove
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — System Info and Security Tips features; not used for advertising or unrelated tracking
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) — display Bluetooth on/off state on the System Info screen; not used for advertising or unrelated tracking
