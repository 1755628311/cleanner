Privacy Policy

NeatCleaner · Package: com.hb.cleaner

Last updated: August 24, 2026

NeatCleaner (“NeatCleaner”, “we”, “us”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app may process, why it needs certain permissions, and how we handle your data.

1. Information We Process

To provide storage insights, cleanup suggestions, and optimization features, the app may read the following information locally on your device:

  • Device storage usage and available free space
  • App cache sizes and temporary files in accessible directories
  • Installed app list (for counts, app management shortcuts, and uninstall requests)
  • Photos, videos, audio, and other media files when you use media-related features
  • Large files, duplicates, and other items identified during scans you initiate

By default, scan results are processed and displayed on your device. We do not upload your personal files or scan results to our servers unless a specific feature you use explicitly requires it and we disclose that at the time of use.

2. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the app may request the following permissions. We ask for permissions only when needed and explain their purpose in the app:

  • Storage / Media access – to scan and clean cache, temporary files, and media content
  • All files access (Manage external storage) – to perform deep cleaning on devices where scoped storage limits regular access
  • Usage access – to show app usage statistics and identify reclaimable cache
  • Network state – to check connectivity for optional online features
  • Request delete packages – to help you uninstall apps you choose to remove

You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings. Some features may not work without the relevant permission.

3. How We Use Information

  • Display storage summaries and cleanup recommendations
  • Perform cleaning, compression, or deletion actions you confirm
  • Remember your in-app preferences (such as language settings)
  • Respond to feedback you send us

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share local scan results with third parties except:

  • When required by applicable law or valid legal process
  • When necessary to provide a feature you actively initiate and we clearly disclose that sharing

5. Data Retention

Scan results are used for on-device display and cleanup during your session. Local preferences are stored on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

  • You may decline the privacy consent dialog and exit the app
  • You may revoke permissions at any time in Android settings
  • You may clear app data to remove local preferences

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy. The latest version is available in the app and at this page.
